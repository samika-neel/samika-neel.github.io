Dressing for the weather

Crisp, pleasant days and distinctly chilly nights — highs of about 25°C and lows of around 12°C.

  • Daytime — light, breathable ethnic or casual wear.
  • Evenings — warm layers, a jacket, shawl or stole for the outdoor events.
  • Footwear — the ceremonies are on lawns and gardens, so block heels or flats will serve you far better than stilettos.
