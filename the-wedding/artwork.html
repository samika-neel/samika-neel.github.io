The Artwork

The Toile

The custom toile print brings the journey of Samika and Neel to life through a garden of carnations and delicate feverfew, alive with hummingbirds in flight.

At its heart are the hydrangeas, clusters of individual blooms coming together as one, reflecting two lives, two families and many journeys now intertwined.

A garden of their story, rooted in friendship, shaped across continents, now growing together.
