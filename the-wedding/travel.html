The Venue

Namah Resort by Radisson Individuals

Nestled against the Shivalik foothills, Jim Corbett National Park is India's oldest national park — a tranquil escape of lush greenery and riverine landscapes. Namah is a riverfront retreat with a large pool, a spa, a fitness centre and landscaped gardens.

Getting There

By air

  • Nearest airport — Pantnagar, about 80 km away (roughly a 2-hour drive)
  • Alternative — Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun, about 200 km away (roughly a 4.5 to 5-hour drive)

By rail

  • Nearest station — Ramnagar Railway Station, about 15 minutes from the resort

By road

From Delhi Delhi › Ghaziabad › Hapur › Gajraula › Moradabad › Kashipur › Ramnagar

From Dehradun Dehradun › Haridwar › Najibabad › Kashipur › Ramnagar

Our tip Leave Delhi around 6:00–7:30 AM. An early start helps you beat the NCR traffic and makes the journey considerably more relaxed.

Along the Way

  • Shiva Tourist Dhaba, Hapur — a reliable first stop for authentic parathas and classic dhaba tea.
  • Haldiram's or Haveli, Gajraula — clean restrooms and a wide variety of hygienic, family-friendly food.
  • Udupiwala, Kathgodam / Kashipur route — great for a light South Indian meal before the final stretch.

Weather at Jim Corbett

The final week of November brings crisp, pleasant days and distinctly chilly nights, with temperatures typically ranging from a high of 25°C to a low of 12°C. We recommend light, breathable ethnic or casual wear for the daytime festivities, and warm layers, jackets or shawls to stay comfortable during the outdoor evening events.
